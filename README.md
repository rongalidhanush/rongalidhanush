## 📚 Research & Publication

### SVD-Based Image Compression: A Comprehensive Survey

**Published in IEEE Access · 2026**

My research explores Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)-based image compression methods and their development across conventional, hybrid, optimization-assisted, and learning-assisted approaches.

**Research highlights:**
- Analysis of SVD-based image compression techniques
- Exploration of hybrid SVD-DCT and SVD-DWT approaches
- Study of optimization-assisted and learning-assisted methods
- Comparison using Compression Ratio, PSNR, SSIM, and MSE
- Discussion of computational complexity and emerging research directions

📄 **DOI:** [10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3694419](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3694419)
